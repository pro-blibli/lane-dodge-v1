SETTINGS

A built-in psytrance loop plays by default — upload your own track to override it.
The built-in loop is generated live in the browser (Web Audio), so there's no file to load and nothing to license.

HOW TO PLAY

Dodge across 3 lanes. Survive as long as you can.

CONTROLS

  • ◀ / ▶ buttons, or ← / → arrow keys, to switch lanes
  • 💥 red button fires your blaster at whatever's in your lane

DIFFICULTY

  • Level 1 — single-lane blocks only, slow
  • Level 2 — double-lane blocks mixed in, a bit faster
  • Level 3+ — zigzag obstacles start appearing, and speed keeps climbing every 30s

POWER-UPS

  • Shield — absorbs one hit (stacks up to 3)
  • Slow-Mo — halves obstacle speed for a few seconds; you briefly blink and phase through obstacles right after it ends
  • 2× Score — doubles points for a few seconds
  • Blaster — gives 2 bullets; fire with the red button to destroy the nearest obstacle in your lane
